# Canary Wharf near-ground fields 027

The city viewer now includes true 4 m SCALED wind sampled at 2 m height, a controlled planar road tracer, clear-sky sunlight/shadows and a terrain-limited rain flooding scenario. The original detailed city mesh, traffic and 600-UAV / 100-route network are preserved. Each field has its own clock. Temperature remains the earlier 32 m run forced by the earlier physical wind; it has not been recalculated from the surrogate wind.

The wind input is rebuilt directly from retained native geometry and 12,668 outer building footprints. Its grid is 64 × 1024 × 1024 at 4 m, origin [-2048,-2048,-4] m. The forced numerical ground layer lies below local zero, so the first atmospheric cell center is 2 m. This vertical model alignment is explicitly unvalidated; the top clearance above the highest source building is only about 18.46 m. No old 8 m or 16 m occupancy or flow is enlarged. The viewer crops exact cells to the 4 km square.

SCALED strict pretrained weights produced 100 latent steps and decoded steps 80, 90 and 100. The underlying 100 s/model-step interpretation is not a calibrated physical clock; the viewer labels model steps. Encoding took 16.8 s, full inference/three decodes 107.4 s, peak GPU allocation 24.44 GiB. All values are finite, blocked velocities are zero and final slices equal the retained full volume exactly. The final 2 m speed mean is 0.2184 m/s, P95 0.4761 m/s; these are surrogate outputs, not a preserved 5 m/s atmospheric inlet. Wind particles use 1× vector speed instead of the previous 60× display acceleration.

Pollution is a 2-D conservative passive tracer driven by the final frozen horizontal wind, on 4 m road-source cells. Uniform 0.01 a.u./s emissions, diffusion 1 m²/s and a 600 s duration are controlled assumptions, not an inventory, 3-D dispersion or health exposure model. Positivity, no-through-building transport and volume-integrated mass accounting are checked.

Sunlight uses the actual 4 m geometry at 12:00–12:10 UTC on 21 June 2026 (13:00–13:10 BST), with 11 irradiance and shadow frames. Solar angle, isolated obstacle shadow and brute-force reference checks passed. This is a clear-sky scenario.

Flooding uses licensed EA measured terrain only in the approximately 1 km central patch. After water/building exclusions, 37,352 cells are valid; the other 962,648 displayed cells are explicitly invalid. A controlled 50 mm/h, 600 s rain event gives a maximum depth about 0.4145 m. Relative mass residual is 9.43e-8; negative-depth clamping accounts for 0.0473% of rainfall. There is no calibrated drainage, infiltration, tide or roof runoff. Unknown terrain remains blank, not dry or safe. Depth is overlaid on a flat presentation plane; this is not a surveyed 3-D flood surface or hazard forecast.

Retained protocol runs: canary_wharf_geometry4m027, canary_wharf_wind4m027, canary_wharf_pollution027, canary_wharf_sunlight027, canary_wharf_flood027 and canary_wharf_city_fields027. Scientific arrays, masks, original inputs and code/weight provenance are retained; browser assets contain only selected display arrays. Versioned physics027 URLs avoid stale cached fields.

Validation: protocol asset checks passed; browser tests compare all four new field arrays against recorded NPY values with zero difference, verify invalid-cell transparency and independent clock labels, and exercise shadow mode. Two tracer conservation/obstacle tests and seven viewer/clock tests passed.
