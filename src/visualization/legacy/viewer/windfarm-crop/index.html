This test preserves all turbine locations and the scene terrain. Rotor dimensions come from the visualization model, not manufacturer measurements. Inflow is assumed along +x at 8 m/s above the terrain, with all rotors facing the wind and C′T=4/3. Axial actuator-disk forces replace static blades; towers and nacelles are retained. There are no resolved rotating blades, torque, turbulence closure or electrical power. The computational grid is 4 m, while the original DSM is about 30 m; this adds no terrain measurement accuracy. This is a 10-second startup test. Local peak velocities exceed the inlet significantly and require further numerical checks. Colours saturate outside the displayed range; hover for raw values. Steady state and engineering accuracy remain unverified.
